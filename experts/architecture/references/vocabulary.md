# architecture: vocabulary and practices

Course pointers are to NCARB088-03 "Predesign Planning Part 10: Predesign Plan" by slide; definitions are paraphrased.

## Vocabulary
- Predesign plan / architectural program: the document setting out values, goals, facts, needs and ideas for a project [slides 188, 192]
- Values, goals, facts, needs, ideas: the order a program is built in; needs derive from values, goals and facts [slides 10, 60]
- HECTTEAS: the course's eight value areas (human, environmental, cultural, technological, temporal, economic, aesthetic, safety) [slides 39, 190]
- Performance requirement (PR): how a space, system or material must perform, stated so it can be judged yes/no or measured [slides 63, 64]
- Design requirement (DR): a physical characteristic (size, shape, relationship, product); usually derived from a PR [slides 70, 72]
- Net-to-gross ratio / building efficiency / grossing factor: assignable area over gross area, and the unassigned share added to reach gross [slides 88, 90, 94]
- Relationship matrix / relationship diagram: a grid of every space against every other / one-space-at-a-time bubble diagrams of known relationships [slides 104, 106, 110]
- Space planning sheet: a per-space requirement sheet (purpose, area, users, activities, furnishings, relationships) for design development [slides 118, 121]
- Executive summary and sign-off: the short page an owner's executive signs, with a preliminary-plan disclaimer (design-build-delivery and ae-practice hold its uses) [slides 31–35]
- Design ideas, programmatic concepts, precepts: owner, user or planner ideas recorded as ideas, not requirements [slides 162, 170, 174, 176]
- Program confirmation: the architect's review of the owner's program as the first design task [slide 177]
- Design release (Indiana): state or certified local approval to build, issued on plans by a registered architect or engineer with a sworn code-compliance certificate [IC 22-15-3-1, -2]
- Area measurement standards: BOMA's per-building-type standards and its gross-areas standard (ANSI/BOMA Z65 series) [boma.org]

## Practices
1. Problem seeking (Peña and CRS): programming as analysis kept separate from design as synthesis; work-session cards [slides 39, 178]
2. Values-first predesign planning (Hershberger): values found before facts and needs, in a value matrix [slides 10, 40]
3. Performance-based versus prescriptive needs (Duerk): a mix of goals, PRs and DRs, choosing per need [slides 63, 77]
4. Phase-staged programs: separate content for master plan, schematic design and design development [slides 16, 58]
