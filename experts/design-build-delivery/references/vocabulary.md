# design-build-delivery: vocabulary and practices

## Vocabulary
- Design-build (DB): one contract for design and construction; a single point of responsibility [FHWA PDB flyer]
- Progressive design-build (PDB): design-builder picked early on qualifications; design is developed with the owner; price is agreed later [DBIA BP 2023 Procuring BP4 a–c]
- Phase 1 / Phase 2: PDB pre-price services, then the design-and-construction contract (CD 400, then CD 410 or 415; CD 411 uses Phase 1/2) [ConsensusDocs 2023-03-07, 2025-11-19]
- Validation, Preliminary Design, Final Design and Construction: DBIA's PDB service steps [DBIA BP 2023 Procuring BP4 b]
- GMP / GMP amendment: guaranteed maximum price, set later by amendment (CD 410.1, 411.1); CD 411 allows several progressive GMPs [ConsensusDocs 2023-03-07, 2025-11-19]
- Off-ramp: the owner's exit when the parties cannot agree on scope, schedule, design or price [DBIA BP4 d; ConsensusDocs 2023]
- Earned value of DB services: what the design-builder is paid on an off-ramp [DBIA BP4 d]
- Performance vs prescriptive requirements: the end result wanted vs a predetermined design solution [DBIA position statement]
- Bridging: the owner sets substantial prescriptive requirements, amounting to preliminary design, before selecting a design-builder [DBIA position statement]
- Spearin: the doctrine DBIA cites for prescriptive requirements shifting design risk back to the owner [DBIA position statement]
- Two-step best value: Step 1 RFQ shortlist (≤3), then Step 2 proposals [DBIA BP3 b]
- Stipend: payment to unsuccessful shortlisted proposers, announced at RFQ [DBIA BP4 e]
- QBS: qualifications-based selection [DBIA BP4 a; Franz et al. 2017]
- Open-book / fully transparent pricing: owner sees cost development [DBIA BP4 a; VDOT CTB 2022]
- Design professional of record (DPR): the licensed designer on the design-builder's team [DBIA BP 2023 Contracting BP3 a]
- Early work package (EWP): construction released before full design [FHWA PDB flyer]

## Practices
1. Two-step best-value DB: RFQ, shortlist of at most three, then priced technical proposals with a stipend. Gives price certainty at award [DBIA BP 2023 Procuring BP3]
2. Progressive DB: QBS, phased services, price after agreement, a defined off-ramp. Its share is growing on projects with technical complexity, cost volatility and evolving scope [DBIA BP 2023 Procuring BP4; DBIA PDB primer announcements 2026]
3. Contract structures: CD 410 + 410.1 (single phase, cost plus fee with GMP); CD 400 then 410/415 (two-phase PDB); CD 411/411.1 (progressive GMPs); AIA A141 with B143 downstream; CD 420 downstream [ConsensusDocs; AGC chart 2017]
4. Early work and phased permits to start construction while later design continues [FHWA PDB flyer; ConsensusDocs 2025-11-19]
