# ae-practice: vocabulary and practices

## Vocabulary
- Responsible charge / responsible direction and supervision: the registrant who directs and answers for the engineering work; required of a full-time PE employee for a business to practice in Indiana [IC 25-31-1-18(b)]
- Offer to practice: holding the business out as providing engineering; barred without the responsible-charge PE [IC 25-31-1-18(b)]
- Seal: the registrant's mark; attests the work is theirs and that they take full professional responsibility [IC 25-31-1-16(b)]
- Engineer of record (EOR): the engineer responsible for the permanent design [FMI Q1 2019]
- Design-builder qualification: being, employing, or teaming with a person qualified for the design and construction services, tested when the services are provided [IC 5-30-3-1]
- CPrL: contractor's professional liability; claims-made; covers economic loss [IRMI 2012]
- CG 22 79 / CG 22 80: CGL endorsements for means and methods, and for design-build design done on the insured's behalf; the insured's own professional services are excluded [IRMI 2012]
- Insured-vs-insured exclusion: can leave a designer uncovered under an owner-required single policy [FMI Q1 2019]
- Net revenue (NSR): revenue less pass-through consultant fees and direct expenses [AIA, Webber]
- Net multiplier / payroll multiplier / utilization / overhead rate / break-even rate: the four firm KPIs and break-even as one plus the overhead rate [AIA, Webber]
- QA vs QC vs peer review: QA = the firm's process; QC/checking = the design team's own review; peer review = independent and in addition [ASCE PS 351; Schwinger 2025]
- Professional surveyor responsible charge: needed for boundary, plat, monument, description and acreage work [IC 25-21.5-1-7, -9-5]

## Practices
1. Depth spectrum: coordination-only designers, then means-and-methods and temporary works, then engineer of record on the permanent structure [FMI Q1 2019]
2. Delivery structures: in-house department; joint venture with an outside designer; acquisition of a design firm; sister company [FMI Q1 2019; Friedlander c.1996]
3. Insurance: CPrL or a design liability policy covering qualified in-house design staff and subcontracted design, kept apart from the GL program [IRMI 2012; Todaro 2009]
4. QA: reviews in-house from project start, at several checkpoints, by a seasoned reviewer; outside peer review added on critical work [Schwinger 2025; ASCE PS 351]
