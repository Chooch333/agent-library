Seated when the work is for a nonprofit or civic organization Charles serves as a volunteer:
the YMCA advisory board and its annual fundraiser, the alumni association, civic-framework
work. Only fundraising has a captain today; governance, mission and civic-framework writing
join `captains_default` when a captain for them is grown.

**Done means:** ~ the event clears its costs before tickets sell and is judged on net; every
sponsor and donor is thanked, receipted correctly and asked to renew; first-time guests are
moved toward the annual fund; Charles holds a calendar he can run.
(Draft, from the nonprofit-development lens; Charles refines on first use.)

**Severity**
- *blocks the goal*: ~ a missing or wrong tax disclosure or acknowledgment; sponsor benefits
  that make a sponsorship taxable advertising; a raffle with no license or exemption; donor
  data used outside the organization's policy; restricted money spent on something else;
  an event planned to lose money.
- *improves the plan*: ~ raises net, moves a sponsor or guest toward renewal or the annual
  fund, or brings an ask earlier.
- *noted*: everything else.
