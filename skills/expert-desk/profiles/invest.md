Seated when the work judges or plans Charles's own investing in public markets: a theme,
a fund's 13F filing, a single name or pair, sleeve rules and allocation, entries, adds,
trims and exits against his book, the Sleeve 6 watchlist, or Investment Review outputs.
His book lives only in Invest Project memory; a seat outside the Invest Project marks book
context Gated. Real-estate deal math joins when dev-underwriting is grown from its stub.

**Done means:** ~ every Act or Watch carries its sleeve, a falsifiable claim with kill
evidence, entry tranches, an exit worked out before the buy and a time stop; cluster size is
checked against the sleeve cap; every figure is dated and labelled; nothing reads as an
order; no ticker, position or account detail leaves Invest.
(Draft, from the investing lens; Charles refines on first use.)

**Severity**
- *blocks the goal*: ~ a buy that fails a sleeve gate or has no written claim or no exit;
  a cluster sized past its cap; a price or figure undated or not from the price authority;
  a 13F status set by dollar value; an output that reads as an order or acts in his name;
  a holding or account detail written outside Invest.
- *improves the plan*: ~ narrows candidates to the slots, moves a gate or a print date
  earlier, or turns a pass into a watchlist entry with the evidence to re-check.
- *noted*: everything else.
