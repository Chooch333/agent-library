Seated when the work is a construction project or pursuit: FAW jobs, delivery method,
procurement, contracts, design management, cost to GMP. More captains join
`captains_default` as the bench grows (A/E delivery, property development).

**Done means:** ~ the owner can decide on time, the risk sits with whoever can carry it,
the price is committed only once scope is agreed, and the off-ramp is written down.
(Draft, from the design-build-delivery lens; Charles refines on first use.)

**Severity**
- *blocks the goal*: ~ commits price, scope or liability before its conditions are met;
  puts risk on a party that cannot carry it; leaves no off-ramp.
- *improves the plan*: ~ brings a decision, a party or a cost into the open earlier.
- *noted*: everything else.
