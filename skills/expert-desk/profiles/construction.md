Seated when the work is a construction project or pursuit: FAW jobs, delivery method,
procurement, contracts, design management, cost to GMP, or building the builder's in-house
design practice. More captains join `captains_default` as the bench grows (property development).

**Done means:** ~ the owner can decide on time, the risk sits with whoever can carry it,
the price is committed only once scope is agreed, and the off-ramp is written down.
~ For practice-building work: the practice can legally offer, seal and insure what it sells
before it sells it, and its fee clears break-even.
(Draft, from the design-build-delivery and ae-practice lenses; Charles refines on first use.)

**Severity**
- *blocks the goal*: ~ commits price, scope or liability before its conditions are met;
  puts risk on a party that cannot carry it; leaves no off-ramp; offers or seals design
  before licensure, the sealing PE or the professional liability cover is in place.
- *improves the plan*: ~ brings a decision, a party or a cost into the open earlier.
- *noted*: everything else.
