2. **Where the work departs from our way**: line · what the work does instead · seat
3. **Blockers**: finding · seats
4. **Changes proposed**
5. **Where seats disagree**
6. **Questions for Charles** (≤3)
7. **Newer than the briefs:** N lens-notes filed on expert-bench, or none
8. **Noted:** N items (in the papers below)
9. **Seats:** list with mode · **Left out / dropped:** expert, and why
10. **Writes during the run:** none, or what, where and when (step 5's check; the lens-notes in line 7 are expected)
