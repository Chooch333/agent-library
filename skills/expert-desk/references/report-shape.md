# Report shape

**Expert Desk: <situation in a line>** · Goal: <one line> · Mode: <A | A.1 | B>

1. **How the room would do it**: one A-to-Z path (two only at a real fork), each step · seat · Our way line or record behind it; *~ new* where nothing is
2. **Where the work departs from our way**: line · what the work does instead · seat
3. **Blockers**: finding · seats
2. **Options the room added**: option · trade · seat
3. **Changes proposed**
4. **Where seats disagree**
5. **Questions for Charles** (≤3)
6. **Noted:** N items (in the papers below)
7. **Seats:** list with mode · **Left out / dropped:** expert, and why
8. **Writes during the run:** none, or what, where and when (step 5's check)
